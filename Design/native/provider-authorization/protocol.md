## Contract to establish

A request belongs to its original account, route, model and routing preference. Disconnect, credential replacement, mode/client/scope/endpoint/model changes, change-then-revert and reset invalidate suspended preparation and unsubmitted packets. A stale authorization-code callback or refresh must not restore disconnected credentials or overwrite a replacement account. Invalidated, failed or unpersisted credentials must not be returned for submission. Concurrent valid refreshes need a defined ownership/coalescing policy. A final guard after serialization/session setup must cover every compatible coach/planning/vision route. Accepted replies must identify the actual captured provider route rather than always claiming OpenAI. Already submitted requests cannot be recalled; response acceptance after changes needs an explicit factual outcome.

Credential read/write/delete outcomes must be checked, including missing versus protected/unavailable data. A failed replacement must preserve the prior credential rather than delete it before adding another. Sign-in sessions need their own identity, callback/state validation and cancellation policy. Do not invent live Google/native OAuth compatibility from synthetic checks. Reset/restore and direct defaults/keychain access need auditing against the same identity.
