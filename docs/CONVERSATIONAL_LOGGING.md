# Health entries from a conversation

In Assistant, choose **Review health entries** under one of your own messages. The review shows the original message, saved and pending counts, and separate drafts. Open each draft, check its time and fields, then choose **Confirm and save entry**. Sending a message or receiving a reply does not save observations automatically. Assistant replies and plans cannot be used as the source of this action.

Supported entries are symptoms, energy/mood/stress check-ins, water and workouts. You can add an entry yourself, remove an unsaved draft, and change every proposed value. Time starts at the message time; adjust it for an earlier event. Ratings stay blank unless explicitly provided. Workout energy remains unknown when not provided; this flow does not calculate calories from activity duration. Symptom duration uses whole minutes. Notes start empty; the original message is stored separately as read-only provenance.

Local drafts recognize a deliberately small set of explicit statements, including “I drank 250 ml of water,” “I walked for 20 minutes,” “I have a headache 5/10,” and “Energy 2/5, mood 4/5, stress 3/5.” Negation, questions, plans and hypothetical/quoted examples suppress this local extraction. Unsupported wording can still be entered manually. This parser is not general language understanding.

If an AI provider is available, **Suggest entries with AI** can propose up to 12 entries from the selected message. Remove unsaved local drafts first. Only that message is sent for this request; no unrelated health history or records are added. The configured provider is shown. Suggestions require a literal evidence excerpt. Unknown types, malformed output and absent evidence are rejected. Numerical suggestions need matching explicit quantities/units in the excerpt; unstated ratings, symptom names and locations are cleared. Supported quantity conversions include litres to ml, hours/seconds to minutes and kJ to kcal. These checks do not prove the medical or semantic truth of model output: review every field. No model can save, delete, prescribe or change medication through this flow.

AI suggestions are offered before saving from that message. Once entries are saved, the review shows them and allows additional manual entries. **Stop suggestions** or closing the sheet cancels the request and ignores late results. Provider inference and cancellation on signed hardware/external accounts remain separate verification gates.

**Close** discards unsaved drafts and keeps saved entries. If a pending draft was edited, Close and swipe dismissal ask whether to discard those changes or keep reviewing. A saved entry is immediately removed from the pending list. Each proposal has a stable record ID for its lifetime; repeated confirmation checks that ID before writing. Reopening a message with saved entries shows its saved records instead of recreating local proposals. Adding another entry is an explicit action.

Each confirmation atomically writes one domain before changing in-memory state. A storage error leaves the draft open with a retry message. Entries are saved locally in Lumen, without HealthKit write-back. They update Journal, Today and assistant context through the existing health stores. Assistant context can include bounded, deduplicated original-statement excerpts, labelled as reference material; confirmed structured values take priority over source wording corrected during review. The original statement, conversation/message IDs, preparation provider and confirmation time stay with the entry, including after a conversation is deleted. Deleting a conversation discloses that confirmed entries and source excerpts remain. Full app erasure removes them; per-domain editing/deletion controls continue to apply. Water and workout details, symptom editing and check-in editing expose provenance.

Backup version 1 retains this metadata through optional fields. Existing records without provenance continue to decode. Restoring a backup does not grant sharing consent or enable providers. Live provider output, denied permissions, physical-device performance and accessibility text sizes are not established by simulator tests.

The sheet's swipe protection uses Apple's [dismissal decision](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiadaptivepresentationcontrollerdelegate/presentationcontrollershoulddismiss(_:)) and [attempt callback](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiadaptivepresentationcontrollerdelegate/presentationcontrollerdidattempttodismiss(_:)), preserving the existing SwiftUI presentation callbacks. The installed UIKit SDK documents both callbacks from iOS 13 onward; the app targets iOS 18 or newer.


## Correcting a confirmed water or workout entry

Open the entry in Journal or its workout detail and choose **Edit entry**. Change the recorded date/time, amount or duration, optional workout energy, and notes, then choose **Save changes**. Blank workout energy stays unknown; an explicitly entered zero stays recorded. Cancel and swipe dismissal protect edited drafts. Invalid values cannot be saved. Saving replaces the record with the same ID after checked storage succeeds; it does not append a duplicate.

The original statement, preparation source and confirmation time remain unchanged. An edit time is saved separately. If the statement says 250 ml and you correct the entry to 750 ml, the current structured value takes priority in Today, historical totals and assistant context. Source excerpts are references, not a way to recreate deleted observations.

**Delete entry** asks whether to delete or keep the entry. Deletion removes only that Lumen record. The conversation remains, and separate Apple Health/import records can still contribute to your totals. Missing totals become unknown rather than a fabricated zero. These controls are offered for locally confirmed entries; external workouts stay managed by their source. Neither correction nor deletion writes to Apple Health. Existing quick-water, meal and weight logging retain their separate optional write paths.

Version-1 backups preserve optional edit timestamps and provenance. Restoring an older backup can re-add an absent entry under the existing restore-missing-records rules; deletion does not create a permanent tombstone across backups.
