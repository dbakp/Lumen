# Health updates between visits

Lumen can request background Apple Health updates from **Connections → Background Health updates**. The preference starts off. It does not grant Health access: connect/review Apple Health separately. Turning it off requests that HealthKit stop background delivery; foreground refresh remains available. Removing Lumen's local account stops its observers and requests background delivery be disabled before erasing local data. Received/applied times and the in-memory preference are cleared too. Health records in Apple's app are not erased.

Lumen asks for hourly delivery for its supported observed types. iOS controls actual scheduling, supported frequencies and available execution time. Updates may wait for an unlock. This is not continuous monitoring, an emergency service or proof that every data type is readable. Completing the authorization sheet never proves read permission. A denied/empty read stays distinct from an observed deletion.

## What the app does

- Installs observer queries in the application delegate at launch, including a Health-triggered cold launch. Protected data must be available before creating the domain stores; a launch before unlock does not load defaults over unreadable history. It observes the 17 quantities used by the current snapshot, stand hours, sleep and workouts.
- Coalesces events into one serialized refresh. An event during a refresh requests a further pass; a foreground caller retains its request to refresh configured providers. Observer work does not initiate Strava/OAuth synchronization.
- Uses fixed-predicate, paged anchored queries for sleep and workouts, including the initial onboarding connection. The old unlimited sleep fetch and 300-workout refresh cap are removed. The first cache starts 730 calendar days before its creation; subsequent queries reuse that start and the saved anchors. The current cache does not silently move the predicate with the date. Completing a reviewed Health sheet durably requests a cursor reset, preserving cached records/deletion ownership while re-reading newly available historical samples. The system may limit the dates it shares; an empty read does not establish that a history range was granted. If you change sharing in the Health app, use **Review Health access & sync** in Lumen to request a fresh history scan. Each query requests at most 500 samples; deleted objects are processed too.
- Atomically saves each page's anchor together with the sleep/workout records it describes in protected, device-local `healthSync.json`. Failed saves do not advance the durable cursor. A later run replays that page idempotently.
- Derives measured sleep nights from cached sample intervals, unioning overlaps and using stable sample identity. Known deleted samples can change or remove their managed night. Manual nights remain local. Cached Health workouts retain their UUID; explicit tombstones remove only matching live Health records, preserving manual and separately imported entries.
- Applies checked sleep/workout writes before publishing those arrays. A local storage revision check rejects a result prepared across another local edit. If the process stops between writes, the durable cache remains replayable and the next run derives both arrays again. Known managed sleep identities and workout tombstones remain in the device cache so an older restored backup cannot immediately resurrect those known deletions.
- Refreshes current quantities through existing aggregate/statistical queries, preserving Apple's aggregation rather than summing a second raw-sample replica. Those readings retain their separate source/query/date evidence. History charts continue to query Health when opened.
- Acknowledges each observer completion once, including errors, stopped connections and background expiration. A single anchored query has a 20-second timeout. Background history work checks a 20-second budget between pages and retains completed pages for continuation; a foreground refresh can finish a longer backfill. iOS may interrupt work sooner.

Delivery status describes registration, not observed read permission. **Health check triggered** means an actual observer callback arrived; it can arrive in the foreground. **Sleep & workout check** records when both paged domains were checked and applied, including empty results. **Check Health now** requests another check; an error instead offers **Retry Health update**. **Lumen refreshed** is the app's general refresh time, including local-only refreshes; it is not a claim of a Health measurement or successful background delivery. Per-metric current evidence is the authority for those readings.

The cache allows at most 200,000 sleep records, 200,000 workouts, 200,000 retained workout tombstones and 200,000 managed night identities. Its encoded file is capped at 64 MiB and individual persisted anchors at 1 MiB. Reaching a bound or encountering a malformed cache reports an incomplete update rather than accepting a truncated history. There is no automatic destructive cache reset. General readable exports and restorable backups exclude device-specific cursor/cache state; restored health history is still part of the ordinary domain records. The current-device cache survives a staged restore. Erasing Lumen removes it.

## Verification boundaries

Background HealthKit delivery is not supported by Apple's simulator. Simulator builds report background registration as unavailable on this device rather than reporting a registration success. Logic checks exercise production paging/replay/coalescing and sample reconciliation using synthetic values. The simulator verifies the real native preference, persistence and presentation. An unsigned device build checks the device-only registration APIs compile; it does not establish a signed installation, permission behavior or background execution.

Before claiming device delivery, test a signed iPhone with real authorization choices and deliberately created synthetic Health samples: cold launch after a new sample, update/delete while backgrounded, lock/unlock, partial/denied access, reboot/first unlock, repeated events, long backfill/interruption, local edits during preparation, storage failure and disconnect/erase during a query. Confirm the app's current values, history and widgets update, and inspect actual callback/application times. Test the configured provider path independently.

HealthKit may discard old deleted-object notifications. Lumen cannot infer a deletion from an empty or unreadable query; an old Health record with no retained deletion evidence may remain. The cache initially covers two years rather than the entire lifetime Health database. Background synchronization does not establish clinical validity or release readiness.

## Platform references

Implementation follows Apple's [observer-query lifecycle](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/executing-observer-queries), [anchored changes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkanchoredobjectquery), [deleted-object limitations](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkdeletedobject) and [background-delivery entitlement](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery). The explicit entitlement is present in the iPhone target; physical signing/provisioning remains a release gate.
